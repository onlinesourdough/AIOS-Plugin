# Author the selected direction

Reduce the composition to the smallest surface that completes the audience's
job. Choose hierarchy, density and responsive collapse deliberately. Optical
rhythm matters more than identical cards and spacing everywhere. Use believable
draft copy and non-sensitive data; mark fictional examples. Give a landing page
one clear primary action and a dashboard decision-first labels. Use
[human-writing](../../human-writing/SKILL.md) for substantive copy.

Keep canonical `DESIGN.md` portable and understandable without an editor. Use
the selected project's established format; for a new direction, Google
Design.md's `name`/`version` frontmatter and semantic design sections are a
useful starting point. Record color roles, typography, spacing, shape, layout,
components, density, imagery, responsive behavior and concrete do/don't rules.
Name selected references, rights, unresolved limitations and why the direction
fits. A format or token exporter supplies no universal aesthetic.

Relevant interaction states include hover, active, visible focus, loading,
empty, error, success, permission and offline. Errors belong near their control
or group and must be programmatically associated. Status needs a signal beyond
color. Motion needs an appropriate reduced-motion or suppression behavior.

Direction-only work can finish as `DESIGN.md`; no HTML or editor is implied.
When a preview is selected, make it faithful to the direction, with semantic
structure, useful landmarks, heading order, labels, alt text, keyboard access,
focus and skip navigation. Use selected local assets with their notices. Do not
hide absent behavior behind a decorative screenshot.

Inspect the actual selected surfaces and states. Responsive work should retain
content and actions at desktop and narrow widths, including the equivalent of
320px when applicable; explain exceptions such as data tables or manipulation
interfaces. Check contrast, focus, error association and reduced motion using
available tools and visible interaction. A text assertion or lint marker cannot
prove rendered usability. New source adoption needs proof on the rendered task.

Correct in-scope findings under the existing authority. Preserve the old review
and failed evidence when making a new candidate; record which change invalidated
which proof. Do not weaken a required behavior merely to make a check pass.
