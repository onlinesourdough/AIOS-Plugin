# Select references for a visual need

Use explicit owner constraints first, then current accepted brand truth and
prior accepted production, then task references. Original comparison vocabulary
is a fallback; new discovery resolves a concrete remaining role. Do not browse
libraries when the supplied direction already resolves the task.

Keep caller screenshots, URLs, channels, brand assets and prior productions at
their source. Inspect them in place when authorized; record a locator, kind,
visual role, target surface, revision/hash where available, provenance/rights,
positive and avoid signals, and confidence. Do not copy media merely to keep
evidence. Put the actual selected decisions in canonical `DESIGN.md`; the
reference notes remain subordinate evidence.

Record the useful route in the existing task or brief:

- **Direct:** owner, brand and task evidence resolves the direction without a
  material conflict. Study no unrelated candidates.
- **Discover:** one concrete role is missing, such as motion, data density, or
  an accessible interaction. Check current first-party identity/revision,
  license/reuse, availability/maintenance, accessibility, framework/runtime fit
  and visual purpose. Select at most one primary source and one optional
  adapter for that role; otherwise reject it or report the unresolved gap.
- **Explore:** the direction is unresolved or materially different options
  remain. Compare two compact directions; use three only when the brief needs
  that comparison. Explain the choice and stop when one direction is selected.

For exploration, these original lenses can supply a frame, useful signals and
an avoidance. Show only the relevant options; they are not templates or a
catalog the owner must maintain.

| Lens | Frame and useful context | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet editorial | Whitespace, readable rhythm, one modest anchor; reports and reading surfaces | Tiny type or ornamental emptiness |
| Precise product | Crisp grouping and visible states; apps and operational work | Decorative controls or purposeless density |
| Evidence led | Decision-first number/chart, direct labels, supporting table | Equal-card walls or color-only encoding |
| Narrative frame | One focal idea per slide/frame, paced sequence | Wallpaper and competing messages |
| Warm tactile | Warm neutrals, modest texture, legible craft/process | Fake wear or nostalgia before legibility |
| Cinematic human | Person in context, restrained grade, sparse overlays | Unlicensed likeness or mood replacing meaning |

Dated practitioner material, including X posts, can be a discovery signal for
current practice. Retain author, date, locator and inspectable claim. Follow
consequential claims to first-party evidence; popularity proves neither rights
nor accessibility. Older research may explain historical limitations but does
not prove current model capability. Extracted visual signals remain hypotheses
until rendered inspection and the selected reviewer confirm them.

ADS source research informed these methods, not default dependencies: Google
Design.md supplied portable format/lint/export; elayadesign, no-ai-slop and
taste-skill informed focused intake, specific copy, composition and states;
awesome-design-md supplied optional variety research. Rigid palettes, font/
spacing rules, catalogs, complexity and copied brands were rejected.

HeroUI, Origin UI/Originkit, ThreeUI and DesEngs remain possible research sources,
not installed libraries or accessibility guarantees. OpenPencil is an optional
editor. Recheck any newly selected source's current identity and license rather
than reusing the historical ADS audit as approval. The narrow retained
external-playbook lesson is observable error association; vendor/style rules,
book-derived taxonomies and prompt-only improvement claims were not adopted.
